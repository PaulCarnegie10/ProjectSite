# High-Level Idea
I, along with two other students in my computer science class, were selected by faculty to create an autograder esc website to teach students the APCSP curriculm. This was my sophmore year and it was pretty much all of our first times making a website. We have an in window coding space that compiles python, html, and CSS. We created a login method, and stored students scores individually in a database. We also created an easy way to add and edit lesson content.
