# High-Level Idea
This project started well before I joined Zeynep Temel's research team at CMU. It actually started at cornell as Guadalupe Bernal's Masters project. Hundreds of hours of bee footage was captured. With that, my job was to make a software that, using YOLO models, could track the bees trajectory, tell when they were entering and exiting the hive, and be able to actually train new yolo models in the software to better fit the current footage (if someone with a different setup wanted to use it). There is plenty of descriptive content on the poster I have in this folder, but the photos that should be used I am including as well.
